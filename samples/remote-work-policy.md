# Northwind Robotics — Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed their first 90 days may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to manager approval. Roles that require lab access, such as hardware test engineers, follow a separate on-site schedule.

## Equipment stipend
Remote employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $750 and an annual refresh allowance of $150. Receipts must be submitted through the expense portal within 30 days. Monitors, ergonomic chairs and headsets are eligible; consumer entertainment devices are not.

## Security expectations
Remote staff must use the company VPN, keep laptops encrypted, and lock screens when away. Company data must not be stored on personal devices. Report lost equipment to the security team within 24 hours.

## Working hours and collaboration
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Teams agree on meeting-free blocks and document decisions in the team wiki so that colleagues in other time zones can follow along.
